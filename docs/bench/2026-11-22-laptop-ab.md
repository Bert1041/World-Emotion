## What this shows, and what it cannot

- On this laptop the PR is level with `main` (4x drag p95 6.8–6.9 against 6.8–7.0): weather costs
  0.1–0.2 ms more a frame, the map layer slightly less.
- Every frame is under 7 ms against a 16.7 ms budget even at 4x, so there is no pressure here to
  separate builds that differ by tenths of a millisecond. `fx` is reported to 0.1 ms.
- The gate is the Pixel 6 Pro: drag p95 within 10 % of `main` at 4x CPU. Not yet measured for
  this build on a cool phone.
