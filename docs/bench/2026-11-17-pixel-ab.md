Follows [2026-11-12-pixel-ab.md](2026-11-12-pixel-ab.md). Same method: builds in turns on one
phone, gate matrix, one repeat per round.

## What happened

- The first, cool attempt lost the phone over USB after one pair. `main4-1.json` (cool,
  thermal 0) is from it; its `halo` partner (17.0 ms p95, fx 0.9) reached the log but not a file.
- The drop also leaked the bench's preview server (fixed in `tools/bench/run.ts`: the server is
  stopped even when the phone restore throws).
- The rest ran with `--allow-warm` at 36–40 °C, thermal status 1 → 2. Heat dominates the totals:
  `main` alone went from 16.9 to 34.6 ms p95, and its map layer (`base`, untouched by any of
  this) from 11.6 to 22.9 ms. Only the comparison inside each pair means anything.

With halos off, weather still costs about 1.5x `main`'s: the round cores, drawn as arcs where
`main` draws rects. Next change: while halos are off, cores of the small moods (fireflies,
sparkle, embers) are squares of the same area; mist keeps round cores. **Not yet benched.**

## Start-up (`halo`, warm, 39.7–39.9 °C, thermal 2)

8–9 frames over 33 ms in the first 6 s, the same count as `main` cool on 2026-11-12; the map-load
stall grew to 1.4–1.5 s with the heat. Weather spread inside 6 s on all four loads.
